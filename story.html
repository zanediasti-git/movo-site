Our story

Developed in Denmark. Built for everywhere.

We didn't set out to make another drink. We set out to remove the drink from the equation entirely.

It started with a bottle we kept forgetting

Electrolytes are easy. Remembering the bottle, finding a tap, waiting for a powder to dissolve, then carrying the empty around for the rest of the day — that's the hard part. The product worked. The format didn't.

So we looked at the most convenient delivery format we could find, stripped out everything we didn't want anywhere near it, and filled it with electrolytes instead. No nicotine. No caffeine. No sugar. Just the minerals, in something that fits in a pocket.

  • Formulated and blended in Denmark
  • Produced under EU food-safety standards
  • Every batch measured, mixed, and checked

How we work

Three rules we don't bend

  • Say what's in it

    Every active and its dose is printed on the site and the tin. No proprietary blends, no hiding behind a label.

  • Don't oversell it

    MOVO is a convenient way to take electrolytes. It isn't magic, it isn't medicine, and it doesn't replace drinking water.

  • Make it in Denmark

    Formulation and production stay close to home, under EU food-safety standards, so we can stand behind every batch.
