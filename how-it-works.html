How it works

Three steps. No bottle in sight.

A MOVO pouch sits between your lip and gum and releases electrolytes as it moistens. That's the whole routine — there's nothing to mix, measure, or carry.

The three steps

  1. Step 1

    Pop it in

    Place one pouch between lip and gum.

    Either side, upper or lower — wherever it sits comfortably. You'll feel a light tingle as it settles. That's the pouch starting to release.

  2. Step 2

    Keep moving

    No mixing, shaker, or bottle.

    Leave it in for 15 to 20 minutes while you train, drive, travel, or work. Hands stay free and nothing needs to be cleaned up afterwards.

  3. Step 3

    Hydrate anywhere

    Electrolytes wherever you go.

    When you're done, bin the used pouch — don't swallow it. The tin holds 18 and fits in a pocket, so the next one is always on you.

Using it well

The short version

Four things worth knowing before your first pouch.

  • 15–20 minutes

    That's the sweet spot per pouch. Take it out once the flavor fades.

  • Don't swallow it

    The pouch itself is not meant to be swallowed. Remove it and throw it away.

  • Still drink water

    MOVO tops up electrolytes. It does not replace the fluid your body needs.

  • Up to 4–6 a day

    A sensible daily ceiling for most adults. Spread them out across the day.

⚠

Contains xylitol. Keep away from dogs and other pets. Xylitol is toxic to dogs even in small amounts.
