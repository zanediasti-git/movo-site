Frequently asked questions

The basics

What is MOVO?

MOVO is an electrolyte pouch. It's a small, soft pouch that sits between your lip and gum and releases electrolytes — sodium, potassium, magnesium and L-taurine — while you get on with your day. There's no drink to mix and no bottle to carry.

How do I use it?

Take one pouch from the tin and place it between your lip and gum, upper or lower, whichever is comfortable. Leave it there while you train, travel or work, then remove it and throw it away. You don't need to chew it.

How long do I keep it in?

About 15 to 20 minutes. Most of the release happens in that window, and the flavor fading is a good signal that the pouch has done its job.

Can you swallow it?

No. The pouch itself is not meant to be swallowed. Swallowing a little of the liquid as you use it is normal and expected, but the pouch should be removed and binned when you're finished.

Do you still drink water?

Yes, and this one matters. MOVO delivers electrolytes, not fluid. It's designed to sit alongside your normal water intake, not to replace it. If you're sweating heavily, keep drinking.

Is there a daily limit?

For most healthy adults, up to 4 to 6 pouches a day spread across the day is a sensible ceiling. If you're also using other electrolyte or sodium supplements, count those too.

What's in it

Does it contain nicotine?

No. MOVO contains zero nicotine. It borrows the pouch format, nothing else about it.

Does it contain caffeine?

No. There is zero caffeine in MOVO, so it won't interfere with sleep or stack with your coffee.

Does it contain sugar?

No. MOVO contains 0 g of sugar and 0 calories per pouch.

Why xylitol?

Xylitol gives the pouch a clean, slightly sweet taste without adding sugar, and it doesn't leave the aftertaste some sweeteners do. It's used in small amounts purely for flavor.

⚠

Contains xylitol. Keep away from dogs and other pets. Xylitol is toxic to dogs even in small amounts.

Where is MOVO made?

MOVO is developed and produced in Denmark, in food-grade facilities operating to EU food-safety standards.

Safety

Who shouldn't use MOVO?

MOVO is intended for healthy adults. If you're pregnant or breastfeeding, on a sodium-restricted diet, or managing kidney, heart or blood-pressure conditions, speak to your doctor before using it. The same goes for anyone on medication affecting electrolyte balance.

Can children use it?

No. MOVO is not intended for anyone under 18, and the tin should be kept out of reach of children.

Can I use it during exercise?

Yes — it's one of the reasons the format exists. A pouch stays put while you run, lift or ride, so you're not stopping to drink or carrying a bottle. Use your judgement during very intense efforts where breathing through your mouth makes it uncomfortable.

What if a pouch is swallowed by accident?

A single swallowed pouch is unlikely to cause harm in an adult and will usually pass through. Drink some water and keep an eye on how you feel. If a child or a pet swallows one, or if you notice any unusual symptoms, contact a doctor or vet straight away.
